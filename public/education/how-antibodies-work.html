How Antibodies Work

By Sarah Jenkins Published 2026-09-26 5 min read

When a pathogen enters the bloodstream, the adaptive immune system responds with extraordinary precision. Central to this defense are antibodies, specialized Y-shaped proteins capable of recognizing unique molecular patterns called antigens.

The Anatomy of an Antibody

Each antibody consists of four polypeptide chains: two identical heavy chains and two identical light chains. The tips of the Y form variable regions that match specific antigens with lock-and-key complementarity.
