About The Daybook

The Daybook explains the ideas behind the news. We publish two kinds of stories: Education pieces that walk through how something actually works, and News pieces that explain what happened and why it matters — both written in plain language, without assuming prior expertise.

Our writers are teachers, journalists and subject specialists who care more about a reader genuinely understanding something than about being first to publish it.

Editorial standards

Every article is dated, bylined and updated when the underlying facts change. We link to primary sources where they exist and correct errors publicly rather than quietly.
