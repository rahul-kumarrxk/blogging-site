New Clinical Trial Shows Enhanced mRNA Vaccine Efficacy Against Emerging Variants

Reported by Elena Vance Filed on 2026-09-26 5 min read

A multi-center Phase 3 clinical trial involving more than twelve thousand participants has confirmed significant durability in adaptive immune responses following updated mRNA vaccination.

Researchers tracked antibody levels over a twelve-month observation window, noting sustained geometric mean titers and robust memory T cell recruitment even six months after the booster dose.
